VHOLTAT 0.1.0 - VHOLUME-style parkour movement for STRAFTAT
=============================================================

VHOLTAT replaces STRAFTAT's movement with VHOLUME-style parkour: carried momentum, slides,
wall runs, wall jumps, climbs, vaults and mantles. STRAFTAT's rules, weapons and shooting
are unchanged.

INSTALL (about a minute)
1. Close STRAFTAT.
2. Run VHOLTAT-Launcher-0.1.0.exe.
   - Windows may say "Windows protected your PC" because the launcher isn't signed:
     click "More info", then "Run anyway".
3. It finds STRAFTAT through Steam (or press Browse and pick the STRAFTAT folder).
4. Press Install. It downloads BepInEx (the mod loader) and installs VHOLTAT into
   %APPDATA%\VHOLTAT. Your STRAFTAT folder only gets two small loader files that stay
   switched off, so starting STRAFTAT from Steam is still plain vanilla.
5. Press Play. Start STRAFTAT with the launcher's Play button whenever you want VHOLTAT.

Already use a mod manager or BepInEx? Unzip VHOLTAT-0.1.0-manual.zip into your STRAFTAT
folder instead (it contains BepInEx\plugins\VHOLTAT\VHOLTAT.dll).

MULTIPLAYER
- Everyone in a lobby needs the same VHOLTAT version. STRAFTAT itself turns away players
  whose mods differ, and modded players get their own matchmaking pool, so you never land
  in vanilla lobbies.
- Host and invite through Steam as usual. The host's VHOLTAT settings are used by everyone
  in the lobby.
- If your version doesn't match the host's, a banner tells you which version to install and
  you get normal STRAFTAT movement until you update.

MOVES
- Hold crouch while running to slide; jump out of a slide to keep its speed.
- Jump right as you land to keep your speed and build more (bunny hop).
- Jump next to a wall while holding forward to wall run; jump again to wall jump.
  Bounce wall to wall to build speed.
- Jump at a ledge while holding forward to vault onto it; hold forward into a wall in the air
  to climb it.

KEYS
  F6  switch between VHOLTAT and vanilla movement
  F7  speed / state overlay

SETTINGS
  BepInEx\config\dev.riley.vholtat.cfg inside the install (the launcher's Open folder button
  takes you there). Saved changes apply while the game runs.

UNINSTALL
  Run the launcher and press Uninstall. Your STRAFTAT folder goes back exactly as it was.
