VHOLTAT 0.1.0 - VHOLUME-style parkour movement for STRAFTAT
=============================================================

Everyone in the lobby needs VHOLTAT 0.1.0 (this exact version).

INSTALL (about a minute)
1. Close STRAFTAT.
2. Run VHOLTAT-Launcher-0.1.0.exe.
   - Windows may say "Windows protected your PC" because the launcher isn't signed:
     click "More info", then "Run anyway".
3. It finds STRAFTAT through Steam (or press Browse and pick the STRAFTAT folder).
4. Press Install. It downloads BepInEx (the mod loader) and installs VHOLTAT into
   %APPDATA%\VHOLTAT. Your STRAFTAT folder only gets two small loader files that stay
   switched off, so starting STRAFTAT from Steam is still plain vanilla.
5. Press Play. Always start STRAFTAT with the launcher's Play button to have VHOLTAT.

PLAY TOGETHER
- The host creates a private lobby as usual and invites friends through Steam.
- The host's movement settings are used by everyone in the lobby automatically.
- If your version doesn't match the host's, a yellow banner tells you which version to
  install and you get normal STRAFTAT movement until you update.
- Modded players get their own matchmaking pool, so you never meet vanilla players.

KEYS
  F6  switch between VHOLTAT and vanilla movement
  F7  speed / state overlay
  F9  compare the jump with VHOLUME's exact (floatier) jump

MOVES
- Hold crouch while running to slide; jump out of a slide to keep its speed.
- Jump right as you land to keep your speed (bunny hop).
- Jump next to a wall while holding forward to wall run; jump again to wall jump.
  Bounce wall to wall to build speed.
- Hold forward into a wall in the air to climb it; reach the top to mantle up.

MANUAL INSTALL (instead of the launcher, if you already use BepInEx)
  Unzip VHOLTAT-0.1.0-manual.zip into your STRAFTAT folder (it contains
  BepInEx\plugins\VHOLTAT\VHOLTAT.dll).

UNINSTALL
  Run the launcher and press Uninstall. Your STRAFTAT folder goes back exactly as it was.
