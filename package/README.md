# VHOLTAT

Parkour movement for STRAFTAT: carried momentum, slides, wall runs, wall jumps, wall climbs, mantles,
coyote time and jump buffering. STRAFTAT's game rules, weapons and shooting are unchanged.

Everyone in a lobby needs the same VHOLTAT version. STRAFTAT puts modded players in their own matchmaking pool.

## Keys

- **F6** switch between VHOLTAT and vanilla movement
- **F7** debug overlay (speed, state, boost)
- **F8** switch momentum preset (Vholume / Playtest2)

## Moves

- Hold crouch while running to slide; jump out of a slide to keep its speed.
- Jump within 0.15 s of landing to keep your speed (bunny hop).
- Jump next to a wall while holding forward to wall run; jump again to wall jump.
- Hold forward into a wall in the air to climb it; reach the top to mantle.

All numbers are in `BepInEx/config/dev.riley.vholtat.cfg` and apply live.
